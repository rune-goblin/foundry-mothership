Mothership — Type Pairing Specimen

Same content, five settings. All faces OFL 1.1 or Apache 2.0 — free to bundle.

Ground Body

Judge four things: the uppercase display line (character name, section bars), the small caps-style labels at 10–11px where most of a Foundry sheet lives, the tabular figures in the weapon table, and whether the body paragraph stays comfortable at sheet size. Text is real — pulled from content/books/psg/.

A

Space Grotesk over IBM Plex Sans

Retro-futurist display over institutional body. Space Grotesk is Space Mono redrawn as a proportional face — it keeps the mono's odd, engineered details at display size and drops them at text size. Plex is IBM's corporate face: technical, tireless, built for small sizes and dense tables.

  • OFL 1.1
  • 2 families
  • ~70 KB, 5 weights
  • pairs with IBM Plex Mono
B

Archivo Black over Archivo

One superfamily, two extremes. Archivo is a grotesque built for high performance in small sizes; Archivo Black is the same skeleton at maximum weight — stencilled-on-a-bulkhead headers. The cheapest option to ship and the most internally consistent.

  • OFL 1.1
  • 1 family
  • ~55 KB, 4 weights
  • variable width axis available
C

Chakra Petch over Barlow

The overtly sci-fi option — Chakra Petch is angular and clipped, HUD typography. Barlow underneath is a low-contrast grotesk drawn off Californian highway signage: neutral, slightly condensed, very economical in a stat block.

  • OFL 1.1
  • 2 families
  • ~60 KB, 5 weights
  • reads gamey at large sizes
D

Jost* alone

The honest replacement for the Futura PT blocks just deleted — Jost* is a Futura-alike you can actually ship. One family doing both jobs, geometric and poster-ish, closest to 2001 and to the film-poster end of the genre.

  • OFL 1.1
  • 1 family
  • ~40 KB, 3 weights
  • wide circular forms cost table width
E

Roboto — the control

What ships today, for reference. Not a candidate: it is the default-Android face and reads as an absence of a decision rather than a decision.

  • Apache 2.0
  • currently CDN-loaded
